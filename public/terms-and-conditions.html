Part IV — Data Security & Encryption

Last Updated: 30 July 2026

Our Security Commitment

BlockMyCard takes your data security seriously. We implement industry-leading encryption, monitoring, and incident response practices to protect your card information and personal data.

Encryption & Data Protection

At-Rest Encryption

✓ AES-256 Encryption

All card details and sensitive information stored in our database are encrypted using AES-256-bit encryption, the highest standard used by governments and financial institutions.

In-Transit Encryption

✓ TLS 1.2+ Protocol

All data transmitted between your device and BlockMyCard servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher, ensuring no one can intercept your information during transmission.

Key Management

  • Encryption keys are stored separately from data
  • Keys are rotated on a regular basis
  • Only authorized personnel have access to key management systems
  • Multi-factor authentication required for key access

24/7 Security Monitoring

✓ Continuous Intrusion Detection

Our systems are monitored 24/7 for suspicious activity, unauthorized access attempts, and security anomalies using advanced threat detection tools.

  • Real-time log analysis and alerting
  • Automated response to suspected breaches
  • Daily security posture reviews
  • Rapid escalation procedures for incidents

Incident Response & Notification

If a Data Breach Occurs

In the unlikely event of a security incident affecting your data:

⏰ 72-Hour Breach Notification

We will notify you via email and SMS within 72 hours of discovering a confirmed breach, including:

  • Nature of the breach
  • What data was affected
  • Steps we are taking to secure your data
  • Recommended actions you should take
  • Our incident response and remediation plan

Annual Security Audits

We conduct comprehensive third-party security audits every year:

  • SOC 2 Type II Audit: Validates our security controls, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy
  • Penetration Testing: External security experts attempt to breach our systems to identify vulnerabilities
  • Code Review: All application code undergoes security review before deployment
  • Vulnerability Assessments: Regular scans identify and track security weaknesses

Access Controls

Only authorized personnel can access your data:

  • Multi-Factor Authentication: All staff require MFA to access systems
  • Role-Based Access: Employees only have access to data needed for their role
  • Audit Logging: All data access is logged and reviewed
  • Background Checks: All team members undergo security vetting

Data Backups

Your data is regularly backed up to ensure availability:

  • Daily Backups: Encrypted copies created daily
  • 30-day retention of backup history
  • Geographically distributed backup locations
  • Regular restoration testing to ensure backup integrity

6. Your Consumer Rights

As a consumer using BlockMyCard, you have rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019:

Right to Grievance Redressal: You can file a complaint with BlockMyCard's Grievance Officer or with a Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission if you believe we have provided deficient service.
Right to File a Complaint: File a complaint with your District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission for claims up to ₹1 crore, State Commission for ₹1-10 crore, and National Commission for claims above ₹10 crore.
Right to Compensation: You can claim compensation for deficient service, including damages, loss of opportunity, and mental agony (as determined by the Commission).

How to File a Consumer Complaint

  1. First, contact BlockMyCard: Use our Grievance Redressal Policy (Part III of this document) to file a complaint with us. We aim to resolve within 30 days.
  2. If not resolved, file with District Consumer Commission:
    • Visit consumerhelpline.gov.in
    • Find your district's commission
    • Download the complaint form
    • Submit with supporting documents and fee (₹100-500)
  3. State/National Commission: If claim exceeds ₹1 crore, file with higher commission.
