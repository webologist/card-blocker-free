About BlockMyCard.in

A free, open portal helping every Indian act fast when a wallet is stolen or a phone is lost.

Our Mission

Losing a wallet or having your mobile stolen is stressful enough without also having to hunt for the right bank helpline or the correct process to block a card. In that critical window, most people don't know who to call, what number to dial, or which steps actually stop the damage — and that's exactly the gap this portal is here to fill.

We bring together verified helpline numbers, step-by-step blocking procedures, and reporting pathways across banks, telecom providers, and regulatory bodies (RBI, NPCI, TRAI, NCRP, and I4C) — all in one accessible place. Our goal is simple: when something goes wrong, you shouldn't have to lose more time than the incident itself takes.

Built from personal experience. After a wallet was stolen, our founder found themselves needing to block 8 different cards across several banks — and what should have been a quick, routine task turned into a long, frustrating ordeal. Each bank had a different number, a different verification process, and finding accurate information took far longer than it should have.

Why We Built This

That experience made it clear how much easier this process could be with the right information readily available. A card-blocking emergency shouldn't require you to:

  • Search for the correct bank helpline number
  • Navigate multiple verification steps at each institution
  • Understand the role of different regulatory bodies
  • Figure out which documents or information you need
  • Repeat the same story across 8 different phone calls

Every minute matters in a card-blocking scenario. Fraudsters work fast, and so should you.

What We Offer

BlockMyCard.in is built for speed and clarity:

  • Verified bank helplines — all major Indian banks and credit card issuers in one place
  • Step-by-step blocking guides — exactly what to say, what to have ready, and what to expect
  • Regulatory pathways — how to report to RBI, NPCI, TRAI, and other authorities when needed
  • Multi-language support — accessible to all Indians, regardless of language preference
  • Free to register and use — access all helplines, guides, and reporting pathways at no cost. Optional paid feature for securely storing card details.

For Everyone, From Everywhere

This portal is built for:

  • Individual cardholders — whether you have one card or many
  • Small business owners — who can't afford the downtime of a compromised card
  • Senior citizens — who may find card-blocking procedures confusing or intimidating
  • Families — helping multiple members act quickly in an emergency

Made in India, For India

We understand the unique challenges of the Indian financial ecosystem. Our helplines, procedures, and guidance are specifically tailored to how Indian banks, telecom providers, and regulators work — not a one-size-fits-all guide copied from international sources.

This is built with love for every cardholder, small business, and senior citizen who deserves a faster, simpler way to protect their financial security.

Our Commitment

We're committed to keeping this information accurate, up-to-date, and accessible. If you notice an error, have feedback, or want to suggest a bank or process we've missed, please reach out to us.
