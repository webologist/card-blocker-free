🔒 Trust & Security

Your cards. Your details. Your control.

BlockMyCard exists for one reason — to help you act fast on the worst day, when a wallet or phone goes missing. Here's exactly how we treat what you trust us with, in plain words.

The short version

If you only read this far, you've got the important part.

  • ✓We only ask for what helps you reach your bank quickly — nothing more.
  • ✓We never store your full card number, CVV, PIN, UPI PIN, net-banking password, or OTP.
  • ✓You can view, edit, or delete anything you've saved, whenever you like.
  • ✓We'll never sell your details or hand them to anyone marketing to you.
  • ✓We're an early-stage service, and we'll keep telling you the truth about where we are.

What we ask for

The details that help you act fast

Think of BlockMyCard as a notebook you keep for one emergency — but a notebook only you can open. To help you block a card quickly, we let you store things like:

🏦

Bank & card type

The bank name and whether it's debit, credit, or prepaid.

🔢

Last few digits

Just enough to tell your cards apart — never the full number.

📞

Block-card helpline

The bank's official number to block a lost or stolen card.

📱

Your mobile & email

So you can sign in from any phone if yours is lost.

👪

A family contact

An optional alternate person to reach, only if you choose.

✋

Whatever you choose

You decide what to add. Start with just name, mobile, and email.

What we never touch

The secret parts stay secret

This is the line we don't cross. To block a card, your bank never needs the secret parts — and neither do we.

We do not ask for, and do not store:

None of these are needed to block a card. Ever.

  • ✕ Your full 16-digit card number
  • ✕ The CVV (3 digits on the back)
  • ✕ Your ATM PIN or UPI PIN
  • ✕ Net-banking or card passwords
  • ✕ Any OTP

If anyone — a website, a call, an SMS — asks for these "to block your card," that's a fraud attempt, not a safety step. We will never do that.

You're in control

Your saved details belong to you

Not to us. You decide how much to add, and you can undo any of it in a few taps.

  • 👁See everything you've saved, any time you log in.
  • ✎Change or remove any card or contact in a few taps.
  • 🗑Delete your account whenever you want — your saved details go with it.

How we look after it

Built carefully, and built honestly

We take the trust you place in us seriously, and build with that in mind.

🎯

Only what's needed

We collect the minimum that helps you act in an emergency — and skip everything else.

🔒

Visible only to you

We're building the service so your saved details are tied to your login and meant for your eyes.

🗑️

Yours to remove

Nothing you save is permanent unless you want it to be.

🇮🇳

Built for India

Designed around Indian bank block-card helplines and RBI reporting timelines.

We're still an early-stage service, and we're honest about that. As our security practices are formalised, we'll publish the specifics right here — in language you can actually read — rather than hiding behind a policy no one opens.

Our promises

A few things worth putting in writing

  • NeverWe will never sell your information.
  • NeverWe will never hand your details to advertisers or data brokers.
  • NeverWe will never call, text, or email you asking for a PIN, password, CVV, or OTP.
  • NeverWe will never ask for more than you choose to share.

If something ever claims to be from BlockMyCard and breaks one of these, treat it as a scam — and please tell us.

Staying safe

Beyond BlockMyCard

Because we'd rather you never need us in a panic.

  • Your bank will never ask for your PIN, CVV, or OTP over a call or message.
  • If a card is lost, block it first, then file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
  • Save your bank's official block-card helpline somewhere you can reach it — that's exactly what BlockMyCard is for.

Have a question or a concern?

We'd genuinely like to hear it — good or bad. If something about your data, your account, or our security doesn't sit right with you, write to us.
